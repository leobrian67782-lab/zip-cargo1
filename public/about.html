Our Journey

The ZipCargo Story

From a small courier operation to a global logistics platform.

Founded

ZipCargo is Born

ZipCargo started as a small domestic courier service, built on a simple belief: shipping should be fast, transparent and stress-free for everyone.

Expansion

Going International

We expanded our network to cover international air and sea freight, partnering with airlines and port agents across North America, Europe and Asia.

Technology

Real-Time Tracking Platform

We launched our proprietary tracking platform, giving customers live shipment visibility and automated status notifications for the first time in our market.

Specialisation

Pet Transport Division Launched

Responding to growing demand, we launched our dedicated pet transport division — offering IATA-compliant, door-to-door animal relocation to 150+ countries.

Today

A Global Logistics Platform

Today ZipCargo serves thousands of customers across 150+ countries — from individuals shipping personal effects, to businesses running complex international supply chains.
