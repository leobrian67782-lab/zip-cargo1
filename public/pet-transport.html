Frequently Asked Questions

We recommend booking at least 4–6 weeks before your travel date. Some countries require veterinary certificates to be issued within specific timeframes (e.g. 10 days before departure), so early planning gives us time to prepare everything correctly.

Requirements vary by country but typically include: a microchip (ISO 15-digit), up-to-date rabies vaccination, an official health certificate from an accredited vet, and an import permit for the destination country. We will guide you through everything specific to your route.

Yes — the cargo hold is pressurised and temperature-controlled, the same as the passenger cabin. We only book with airlines that have strong live animal handling records and follow IATA Live Animal Regulations strictly. Millions of pets travel safely by air every year.

Some airlines restrict snub-nosed (brachycephalic) dog and cat breeds such as Bulldogs, Pugs and Persian cats due to breathing risks. Certain exotic species may also require CITES permits. We will advise you on any restrictions specific to your pet during the quote process.

Transit times depend on the route. Direct flights take 4–16 hours for most international routes. Door-to-door including collection, documentation checks and delivery typically takes 3–7 days from departure date depending on destination customs processing time.

Cost depends on your pet's size, weight, breed, origin, destination and required documentation. Prices typically range from $500 to $3,000+ for international routes. Request a free quote and we'll give you a full breakdown with no hidden fees.
