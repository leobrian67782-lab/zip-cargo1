Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shipping with ZipCargo

Use the Track Shipment section on this page. Enter your ZipCargo tracking number (format: ZC-YYYY-XXXXX) and you'll get real-time status, location, and a live route map instantly.

We handle shipments of any size — from small parcels under 1kg to full container loads of 30+ tonnes. There is no upper weight limit for sea or road freight. Air freight is ideal for shipments under 500kg.

Yes. Our customs team manages all import and export documentation, HS code classification, duty and tax calculations, and liaises directly with customs authorities in 150+ countries. This is available as a standalone service or bundled with any freight booking.

Cargo insurance is available for all shipment types and is strongly recommended. Coverage options range from basic liability to all-risk insurance. Ask us for a quote when booking your shipment.

Transit times depend on the service: Express Delivery is same or next day. Air Freight takes 1–5 business days. Road Freight is 2–7 days. Sea Freight is 10–30 days depending on the route. We always provide a confirmed ETA at booking.

Address changes may be possible depending on shipment status and service type. Contact our support team as early as possible — once a shipment is out for delivery, changes may not be possible. There may be a redirection fee.

Prohibited items include hazardous materials, live animals, perishables (without prior arrangement), weapons, and counterfeit goods. Dangerous goods such as lithium batteries require special handling — contact us before booking. Some restrictions vary by destination country.

Fill in the contact form with your shipment details — origin, destination, weight, dimensions, and service preference. Our team responds within a few hours with a competitive, no-obligation quote.
